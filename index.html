I am a Computer Science Ph.D. candidate at ⁠University of Rochester, advised by Yuhao Zhu. Previously, I earned B.E. in Electronic Engineering from ⁠Tsinghua University, where I worked on chip design and FPGA implementation, and M.Sc. in Intelligent Science from ⁠Peking University, where I worked on embodied AI and robot learning with a focus on autonomous driving.

I received the ASPLOS 2025 Best Paper Award and was named a DAC Young Fellow in 2026. I was a Research Scientist Intern at ⁠Meta, working on Transformer-based neural rendering, and a Research Intern at ⁠Pixocial, working on video diffusion.

Research. I am interested in end-to-end hardware-software co-design. My publications span AR/VR, AI, and imaging systems. More details are available on my research page.

I am also an AI maximalist interested in Autoresearch/AI4S, aiming to accelerate scientific discovery by orders of magnitude and unlock research that was previously impossible.
